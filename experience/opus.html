Overview

I joined Opus Asset Management as a Business Analyst in August 2026, working mainly across private equity, life settlements, multi-asset and bond funds. Opus is launching private equity as a new asset class, and my focus is building the analytical foundation for it: the metrics, dashboards and cash-flow projections the team uses to monitor the portfolio and plan commitments.

Alongside this, I automate recurring reporting and operational work, so the team spends less time on manual hand-offs and more time on analysis. The role builds directly on my LP-side private-markets data experience at S&P Global, now from the asset manager's side.
